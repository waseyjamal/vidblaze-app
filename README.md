## What is VidBlaze?

VidBlaze is a free Android video downloader that works on 1800+ websites. Paste any video link or share directly from any app — pick your quality and the video saves to your phone's Downloads folder.

Built by the [Drama Hub](https://dramahubs.stream) team, trusted by 40,000+ users.

---

## Features

- 🌐 **1800+ sites supported** — YouTube, social media, streaming sites and more
- 📥 **Background downloads** — use other apps while videos download
- 🎯 **Quality selection** — 360p, 480p, 720p, 1080p, up to 4K
- 🎵 **Audio extraction** — download audio-only in M4A format
- 📤 **Share-to-download** — share any link from any app directly into VidBlaze
- 🔄 **Auto-retry** — failed downloads retry automatically
- 📋 **Download queue** — manage multiple downloads at once
- 🚫 **No ads** — zero advertisements
- 🔓 **No login** — no account required
- 💧 **No watermark** — clean downloads every time

## How It Works

1. Open VidBlaze and paste any video URL — or share a link from any app
2. Wait for the quality picker to load
3. Select your preferred quality
4. Tap Download — video saves to Downloads/VidBlaze/

## Tech Stack

- **Flutter** — UI framework
- **yt-dlp** — video extraction engine (via Chaquopy/Python)
- **FFmpeg** — video/audio merging for adaptive streams
- **Firebase** — analytics and crash reporting

## Privacy & Permissions

VidBlaze requests only the permissions needed to function:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| INTERNET | Fetch video metadata and streams |
| POST_NOTIFICATIONS | Download progress notification |
| FOREGROUND_SERVICE | Keep downloads running in background |
| READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE | Save videos to Downloads folder |

No location, contacts, camera, microphone or account permissions.
